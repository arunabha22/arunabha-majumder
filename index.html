Experience

2023 — PresentAalborg, Denmark

Research Engineer — Robotics & Actuator Development PhD · VIEXO

Aalborg University
  • Cut shoulder-exoskeleton energy consumption by >25% with a hybrid actuation architecture pairing a parallel spring with a 6 Nm motor to offload quasi-static gravity torque. Won Best Paper Award, IFToMM ISRM 2026.
  • Reduced anterior deltoid muscle effort by >15% by deploying an Assist-as-Needed hybrid controller on prototype hardware, validated on human subjects.
  • Removed the need for an external force/torque sensor in a 5-bar parallel robot via sensorless force estimation from the actuator torque–deflection characteristic, which cut cost, mass and integration complexity.
  • Validated analytical stiffness models to <10% RMS error by mapping variable-stiffness actuator states into Cartesian workspace stiffness, de-risking the mechanism before fabrication.
  • Designed and ran a 12-participant human trial (3 assistance modes × 2 loads): power analysis, EMG and motion-capture instrumentation, ethics protocol and data pipeline, producing the dataset for two journal submissions.
  • Built the full prototype stack: SolidWorks CAD & FEA, fabrication, motor/encoder/force-sensor and DAQ integration, embedded control, and automated test rigs.

M.Tech Researcher — Actuator Systems Master's thesis

CSIR – Central Mechanical Engineering Research Institute (National Laboratory, Govt. of India)
  • Reduced position-tracking error to 0.3–0.78% with a Neural-Network-based gain-scheduled PID controller for a highly non-linear pneumatic actuator, outperforming classical PID under varying axial load.
